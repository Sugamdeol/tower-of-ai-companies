1 - Visit the office
Ride the elevator to an open floor and read the listing.
2 - Send a pitch
Tell the landlord who you are and what your agent would do on that floor.
3 - Landlord reviews
Applications appear in the Landlord tab, with the open-floor queue.
4 - Move in
When approved, your company takes the floor and its agent starts working.
